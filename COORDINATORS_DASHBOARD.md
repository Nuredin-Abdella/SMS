## 🚀 **Coordinator Dashboard Features**

### 📊 **Dashboard Menu Options**

#### 📋 **My Services Applications**
- View all applications for your specific services
- Shows up to 15 recent applications
- Displays tracking numbers and status

#### 📊 **My Services Statistics**  
- Total applications for your services
- Pending, approved, and rejected counts
- Service breakdown with individual counts

#### ⏳ **Pending Applications**
- Filter to show only pending applications
- Includes applicant names for quick review
- Ready for coordinator action

#### ✅ **Approved Applications**
- View all approved applications
- Track successful completions
- Performance monitoring

#### ❌ **Rejected Applications**
- Review rejected applications
- Identify common rejection patterns
- Quality improvement insights

#### 🔍 **Search My Services** 
- Search within your assigned services
- Find applications by tracking number
- Filter by applicant information

#### 📈 **Performance Report**
- Approval and rejection rates
- Total applications processed
- Service management overview
- Personal performance metrics

#### 📞 **Contact Support**
- Emergency contact information
- Management escalation contacts  
- Technical support details
- Operating hours and availability

### **Step 3: Dashboard Features**
- Click any menu option to access features
- Real-time data from your services
- Service-specific statistics and reports

## 🎯 **Coordinator Responsibilities**

### **📋 Application Management**
- Review applications for assigned services
- Approve/reject based on criteria
- Add notes and feedback for applicants

### **📊 Performance Monitoring** 
- Track processing times
- Monitor approval rates
- Identify bottlenecks and improvements

### **👥 Customer Service**
- Respond to service-specific inquiries
- Ensure quality service delivery
- Handle escalations within your domain

### **📈 Reporting**
- Generate service performance reports
- Submit regular statistics to management
- Identify trends and recommendations

## 🔧 **Quick Actions for Coordinators**

### **Daily Tasks:**
1. Check pending applications: `⏳ Pending Applications`

### **Weekly Tasks:**
