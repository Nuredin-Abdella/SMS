## ⚡ **Quick Commands**

- **Login**: Send `email:password` to bot
- **Dashboard**: Automatic after coordinator login
- **Help**: `/help` (shows coordinator commands)
- **Admin**: `/admin` (direct dashboard access)
- **Logout**: Use dashboard logout button

## 📊 **Key Features**

- ✅ **Service-Specific Applications**: Only see your assigned services
- ✅ **Real-Time Statistics**: Live data for your services
- ✅ **Performance Tracking**: Approval rates and metrics
- ✅ **Application Management**: Approve/reject with notes
- ✅ **Coordinator Directory**: Connect with other coordinators
- ✅ **Specialized Reports**: Custom analytics for your role
